ABOUT THIS PLATFORM

Learn. Study. Grow.

B.Tech AIML Student Portal is a student-focused learning hub designed to bring study resources and useful learning tools together in one place. It helps students discover available materials, explore categories, and keep their study resources organized.

Study Material

Browse notes, syllabus, previous-year question papers (PYQs), assignments, and other resources by category. Only material that has been added to the portal will appear here.

Search & New Uploads

Enter a subject, chapter, or resource name in the search box. Check the New Uploads panel near the hero section to see recently added resources.

Useful Tools & Updates

Use the tools and links that are available on the portal. If a resource request option is available, send a request to the administrator for material you need.

How to Use This Website

  1. 1Explore the homepage: Review the categories and resources available on the portal.
  2. 2Search or browse: Use the search box or choose Notes, Tools, PYQ, Syllabus, or Assignments.
  3. 3Open a resource: Click its available file or link. Sign in if the portal asks you to log in.
  4. 4Check new uploads: Visit the New Uploads panel for recently added material. New items appear when they are uploaded.

Note: Available content and features may depend on your login status, uploaded resources, and the website/Firebase configuration.
